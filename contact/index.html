Contact

The simplest way to reach me is by email. I'm based in Philadelphia and welcome thoughtful professional conversations about web development and digital work.

Email

jonathan.haas@gmail.com

Please include a useful subject and enough context for me to understand what you would like to discuss. I do not publish a phone number on this site, but we can arrange a call by email when that is the best next step.

Good reasons to get in touch

I'm happy to compare notes on web platforms, Drupal, managed hosting, Cloudflare, accessibility, deployment workflows, documentation, infrastructure operations, vendor relationships, and the work of leading technical teams inside large institutions. I also enjoy talking with people who are navigating platform modernization or trying to make a complicated web estate easier to operate.

This is my personal website and personal contact address. Messages sent here do not go to the University of Pennsylvania, and I cannot use this channel to provide official university support, statements, admissions guidance, or help with Penn accounts and services. For those needs, please use the appropriate contact published on an official upenn.edu website.

Elsewhere

You can review public code and projects on GitHub, see my professional history on LinkedIn, or find personal posts on Instagram. If you are contacting me about a specific repository or post, including its link will help me respond more quickly.

I read legitimate messages, but I may not respond to bulk sales outreach, unsolicited recruiting sequences, link-placement requests, or requests to publish sponsored content. Please do not send confidential information, credentials, private student information, or security-sensitive details by ordinary email.
