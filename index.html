About

I started at Penn as a senior developer in 2013 and now direct web development in University Communications. I manage the communications side of Penn's websites, from Penn Today and the flagship site to the sites that fall under the President's Center.

My job is part strategy, part plumbing: hosting, deployments, accessibility, and translating between editorial teams, leadership and engineers. I still write code, because that's the fun part.
