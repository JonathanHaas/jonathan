Privacy

This is a small personal professional website. It is designed to provide information without asking visitors to create an account, submit a form, or provide personal information.

Information handled by the site

This site does not include a contact form, user accounts, advertising, or ecommerce. It does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies or build profiles of visitors. Like most websites, the hosting and security provider may process ordinary request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps, and diagnostic or security events. That operational data may be retained according to the provider’s configuration and policies.

Email and external services

If you choose to contact me by email, your message, email address, and any information you include will be processed by the email services used by you and by me. I use that information to read and respond to your message, maintain necessary correspondence, and protect against abuse. Please do not send passwords, payment information, private student records, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

The site links to external services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, the University of Pennsylvania, and Google-hosted font files. When you follow an external link or your browser requests a resource from another provider, that provider may receive standard request information and apply its own privacy policy. I do not control those external services.

Questions and changes

If the site later adds analytics, forms, accounts, or other features that materially change how information is handled, this page should be updated to describe them. The current version reflects the site as published on November 22, 2026. Questions about this notice or requests concerning an email you sent can be directed to jonathan.haas@gmail.com.
