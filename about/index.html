About

I'm a web development leader in Philadelphia who likes durable platforms, capable teams, accessible experiences, and infrastructure that stays boring for the right reasons.

What I do

I direct web development for University Communications at the University of Pennsylvania. I started at Penn as a senior developer in 2013, moved into management, and became director in 2022. My team supports the communications side of Penn's web presence, including Penn Today, the university's flagship website, and sites associated with executive and central communications offices.

The work crosses strategy and implementation. On any given project that can mean planning a platform migration, improving deployment practices, reviewing accessibility, working through hosting and DNS, writing technical documentation, or translating among editorial teams, leadership, designers, vendors, and engineers. I still write and review code because understanding the machinery makes the larger decisions better.

How I work

I value systems that people can understand and maintain. Good web work is not only a launch; it is the documentation, monitoring, governance, and team habits that keep a service useful afterward. My technical background includes Drupal, PHP, JavaScript and Node.js, Python, Cloudflare, Pantheon, GitHub Actions, Docker, Linux administration, and modern accessibility practice. I am also interested in practical, reviewable uses of AI-assisted development.

Before Penn, I built software for commerce, systems integration, and health and fitness organizations, including GSI Commerce and eBay. Across twenty-five years in software, the recurring lesson has been simple: clear communication and thoughtful maintenance matter as much as the initial implementation.
